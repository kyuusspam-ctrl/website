Drop your own files in this folder (exact names, lowercase):

  song.mp3      Background song. A "Music" button in the header plays it on a loop.
                (The button stays greyed out until this file exists.)

  room-bg.jpg   Optional photo for the room. It is blended softly into the wall,
                so a dark, moody photo works best.

Everything else here (icon.svg, fonts/) is part of the app. Leave it alone.
